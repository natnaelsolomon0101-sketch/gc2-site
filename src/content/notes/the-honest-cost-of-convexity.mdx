Every month the overlay does not pay is a month someone asks why it is still on. The
question is fair. The local answer is always to take it off, and taking it off every
time the local answer says so is how a portfolio arrives at its worst month with
nothing underneath it.

The overlay we run is long convexity across everything the firm holds. It costs
something to carry. That cost shows up reliably, in ordinary months, where it is easy
to measure and easy to resent. What it buys shows up rarely, in extraordinary months,
where it is hard to attribute and easy to explain away as luck.

## Why the arithmetic is not the intuitive one

Intuition says: pay a small amount many times, collect a large amount rarely, and the
two roughly cancel. If that were the whole calculation, the overlay would be a coin
flip with fees and we would not run it.

That arithmetic omits what the rest of the portfolio is allowed to do because the
overlay exists. A portfolio that must survive its own tail unaided has to be sized
down. Every position carries a haircut for the scenario where several things go wrong
at once. With a permanent hedge underneath, that haircut is smaller, and the capital
freed up works in every month, not just the bad ones.

So the honest comparison is not hedged returns against unhedged returns at the same
size. It is hedged returns at the size the hedge permits, against unhedged returns at
the smaller size prudence would require. Framed that way the overlay stops looking
like insurance and starts looking like leverage you can actually defend.

## The governance problem

None of this survives contact with a bad quarter unless the decision is taken out of
the desk’s hands. A hedge that can be switched off to improve a period will be
switched off, and it will happen at exactly the wrong time, because the pressure to
remove it peaks when it is cheapest and most needed.

Ours is permanent and sized by risk, not by the desk. That is not a statement about
discipline. It is an admission that discipline is unreliable under pressure, and the
only durable fix is to remove the option.

The cost of convexity is real. It is also the smaller number.
